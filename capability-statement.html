Company Overview

Victory Arms Company LLC is an SBA-certified Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business in Jacksonville, North Carolina, outside MCB Camp Lejeune. VAC supplies small-arms ammunition and tactical equipment by resale and distribution, delivers firearms and marksmanship training to government, law-enforcement and unit customers, and quotes federal supply requirements through DLA DIBBS.

Core Competencies

NAICS 423910 · PSC 1305

Ammunition Supply & Distribution

  • NSN-level sourcing of small-arms ammunition (PSC 1305), supplied by resale and distribution
  • Training ammunition, targets and range consumables
  • Manufacturer and distributor quotes turned around against RFQ close dates
NAICS 611699 · PSC U099

Firearms & Marksmanship Training

  • Rifle, pistol and shotgun marksmanship for government, law-enforcement and unit customers, fundamentals through qualification tables
  • Range operations: range safety officer staffing, range safety briefs, cold-range procedures
  • Programs built to the customer's qualification standard, delivered at the customer's range or a contracted commercial range
NAICS 423990 · DLA DIBBS

Tactical Equipment & Federal Supply

  • Weapon carry cases, drag bags, cleaning kits and maintenance items
  • DLA Land and Maritime and DLA Troop Support quoting through DIBBS (CAGE 02S54)
  • Commercial items quoted to SDVOSB set-asides and full-and-open RFQs
