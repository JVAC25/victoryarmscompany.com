Company Overview

Victory Arms Company LLC is an SBA-certified Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business in Jacksonville, North Carolina, outside MCB Camp Lejeune. VAC supplies tactical equipment, weapon accessories and small-arms ammunition to federal buyers by resale and distribution, and quotes DLA requirements through DIBBS.

Core Competencies

NAICS 423990 · 332994

Weapon Accessories & Maintenance

  • Weapon carry cases, drag bags and hard cases
  • Cleaning kits, cleaning rods and maintenance items
  • Small-arms accessories and replacement items quoted to the NSN and part number
NAICS 423910 · PSC 1305

Ammunition & Range Supply

  • Small-arms ammunition (PSC 1305), supplied by resale and distribution
  • Training ammunition, targets and range consumables
  • Tactical equipment and individual gear
DLA DIBBS · CAGE 02S54

Federal Supply Quoting

  • Electronic quotes on DLA Land and Maritime and DLA Troop Support RFQs
  • Commercial items quoted to SDVOSB set-asides, small-business set-asides and full-and-open RFQs
  • Each quote priced to the RFQ's part number, quantity and delivery date

Differentiators

Veteran-Led

Led by a U.S. Marine Corps veteran with direct experience in weapons, ammunition and equipment accountability for operating and training units.

Set-Aside Eligible

SBA-certified SDVOSB and VOSB. Eligible for SDVOSB, VOSB and small-business set-asides.

Domestic & Allied Sourcing

Domestic and allied sourcing only. No hardware, optics, electronics or software from China, Russia, Iran or North Korea.

One Point of Contact

Every quote is priced and signed by the owner, who stays the contact from RFQ to delivery.
